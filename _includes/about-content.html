Sihan Fu

Building thoughtful systems, and living with curiosity.

I am a PhD student at Zhejiang University, supervised by Chengkun Wei. My work sits around cloud networking and AI infrastructure, and I am especially drawn to systems that make complex ideas feel useful in practice.

Before academia, I interned in foreign-trade companies, export factories, and domestic-trade firms. Those experiences did not just teach me how work gets done, but also how people think, negotiate, and care. That perspective still shapes the way I build, learn, and collaborate.

What I Care About

I enjoy ideas that live at the intersection of engineering and human experience: reliable systems, thoughtful tools, and the quiet details that make technology feel elegant instead of heavy.

My Way of Living

I tend to follow my heart, even when the path does not look entirely rational. Trouble and happiness are both part of the same movement; they remind me that life is not only something to optimize, but also something to feel deeply.
